Fortnite

Genre: Action / Shooter / Survival

Description

Fortnite is where you face off people around the world. You find weapons in chests and use them to fight. This is a shooter game. You can play with your friends. There are different modes that you can play. I gave the game 4 stars because it is very easy to rage. If you are playing with people that rage, they make the game not fun.

Rating: ★★★★

I would recommend this game to people because it's a nice way to by pass time and hangout with your friends. I gave the game 4 stars because it's easy to rage.
